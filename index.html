Why we started

Rework costs more than the drawing.

We spent our careers on the production side of fiber design. The biggest cost we saw was not the price of a drawing set. It was the review cycles after it was delivered.

Revision rounds

Offshore sets often come back four or five rounds before they are usable. We have seen one go back thirteen times.

Permit rejections

A missed city, county or DOT requirement can cost a 30 to 60 day resubmittal cycle on a funded build schedule.

Communication gaps

Questions wait overnight across time zones, and corrections get lost between the people who know the field and the people drafting.

Our approach

Experienced designers, with software doing the repetitive work.

01

Onshore, experienced team

Your work is designed and checked by people who have done fiber design in the field, in US time zones, available when you call.

02

One point of contact

03

Your standards, captured up front

We record your design rules and the city, county and DOT requirements for each job at intake, so permit issues are caught before submittal instead of after.

04

Automation where it is safe

Our tools handle sheet setup, labeling, callouts and moving designs from GIS into CAD. Routing and design judgment stay with experienced designers.

The goal: correct the first time, with turnaround that holds steady as your project volume grows.

Services

Full design packages or any single deliverable.

Fiber network design (GIS)

Routing, hub and cabinet placement, splice points, terminals and drops, fiber counts. Aerial, underground or mixed; distributed or centralized split architectures.

CAD construction drawings

Drawing sets on your template with your layers, blocks, labels and title blocks. Existing GIS designs converted into CAD.

Splice sheets & strand maps

Generated from the same network data as the drawings, so counts and labels match across every document.

Permit packages

Prepared to the specific city, county and DOT requirements of each jurisdiction on the route.

QA/QC review

An independent check of designs produced elsewhere, against your standards and the jurisdiction's rules.

GIS data services

Base maps, data cleanup and utility layers such as water, storm and sewer, prepared ahead of design.

Process

How a project moves through our shop.

  1. 1

    Intake & requirements

    We review the project boundary, start and end points, and your design standards with you directly. This step is always handled by people.

  2. 2

    GIS design

    Our designers lay out the network in GIS against current base data and your rules.

  3. 3

    CAD drawings

    The design moves into CAD on your template. Sheet setup and labeling are automated, so designer time goes to the drawing itself.

  4. 4

    QA/QC

    Every package is checked against your standards and the jurisdiction's requirements before it leaves.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    Files in the formats your team and your platform expect, with your point of contact available for questions and revisions.
